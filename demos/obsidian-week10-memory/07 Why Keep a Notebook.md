# Why keep an engineering notebook?

A notebook is useful beyond today's experiment because the machine, workload, software, and assumptions change. Recording the question, prediction, exact command, configuration, raw evidence, interpretation, and uncertainty lets a future engineer reproduce a result, compare it fairly, and notice when a previous conclusion no longer applies.

Linked notes help preserve relationships: a claim points to evidence; evidence points to an experiment; a decision points back to the claim and its limits. When the evidence changes, the connected notes make it easier to find which conclusions and design choices need another look.

The notebook is not a truth machine. Links organize reasoning; they do not make a weak measurement strong. Every result still needs provenance and a skeptical reader.
