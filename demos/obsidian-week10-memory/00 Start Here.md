# Character Garden? No—Computer Architecture: a living engineering notebook

This portable vault connects one course question to an executable experiment and the evidence it produced on Maise.

Start at [[01 The Question]], then follow the links through [[02 Memory Hierarchy]], [[03 Prediction]], [[04 Experiment]], [[05 Observed Results]], and [[06 Conclusion and Decision]]. The links are ordinary Obsidian links: click one, then use **Backlinks** to see what points back here. Open **Graph view** to see the note network.

The experiment and six run receipts are under `evidence/2026-10-22/`. They are machine-specific observations, not expected answers. The accompanying [[Instructor Runbook]] fits a five-minute demonstration. [[07 Why Keep a Notebook]] explains why the record matters after this experiment ends.

## Evidence rule

Every measured number in this vault points to a saved CSV. A plausible number without a run receipt is not a measurement. Keep the distinction visible: observed result, interpretation, limitation, and next decision are separate things.
