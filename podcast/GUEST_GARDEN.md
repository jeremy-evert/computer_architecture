# Podman at the Podium — Guest Garden

**Status:** Working relationship map, not a booking calendar  
**Purpose:** Preserve people and possible story angles before the ideas evaporate  
**Important:** Verify exact titles, affiliations, preferred names, availability, and topic fit before any outreach. Inclusion here is an idea, not a commitment or endorsement by the person named.

## Invitation philosophy

This show should not collect famous names for decoration.

A guest episode begins with an interesting machine question, result, failure, graph, system, or mystery. Jeremy does enough homework to make the phenomenon worth another person's time. Then the guest is invited because their way of seeing could make the question better.

A useful aspiration is:

> **Earn an hour of a remarkable person's curiosity, then make a fifteen-minute episode worthy of it.**

The final episode may be shorter or longer. The point is to respect the guest's time and edit toward the strongest story rather than publishing every minute because it was recorded.

## How to grow this file

When a new possible guest comes to mind, capture four things rather than writing a biography:

1. **Who is the person?**
2. **What way of seeing do they bring?**
3. **What machine question could earn their time?**
4. **What homework must Jeremy complete before inviting them?**

Do not turn this into a ranked wishlist.

The show is strongest when the right person meets the right mystery at the right time.
