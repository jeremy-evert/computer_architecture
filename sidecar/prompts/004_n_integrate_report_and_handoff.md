# Prompt 004_n - Final integration, acceptance, and handoff

**Status:** OPEN  
**Depends on:** 004_a through 004_m complete enough for review  
**Purpose:** helm-level acceptance of the entire Prompt 004 campaign

## Why this prompt exists

No worker, week, or validation machine gets to self-declare the technical course finished.

004_n is the integration pass that asks whether the thing we built is actually the course described by the parent constitution.

It is deliberately separate from week authoring and platform validation so the final reviewer can judge the whole system rather than defend their own local choices.

## Mission

Review the complete Weeks 5-14 technical core, reconcile all 004_a-004_m reports, resolve blocking contradictions, and write the authoritative Prompt 004 execution report.

Do not start Savnac imprinting here.

- parent Prompt 004;

- authored week packages;
- continuity map from 004_l;

## Final acceptance questions

### Instructional coherence

- Do Weeks 5-14 feel like one investigation?
- Does each week have a clear belief/prediction to refine?
- Does Monday earn Wednesday?
- Does Wednesday generate evidence Friday requires?

### Continuity

- Does Week 5's machine return in Week 14?
- Does the Weeks 6-9 specimen persist?
- Do Weeks 10-11 share a memory story?
- Do Weeks 12-13 share a workload story?
- Do metrics mature rather than simply multiply?

### Evidence quality

- Are important adjectives attached to experiences where appropriate?
- Do authored claims respect what experiments actually measure?
- Are inconvenient results preserved?
- Are plots/data machine-readable and reproducible?

### Dossier

- Is it alive rather than worksheet-shaped?
- Can Week 14 genuinely defend decisions from accumulated evidence?
- Is the final PDF build proven?
- Does it freeze at Week 14?

### Access/equity

- Required path no-paywall?
- CPU-only?
- no required premium AI/agent?
- no required GPU/private network?
- same grading ceiling through fallback evidence?
- support matrix honest?

### Assessment

- weekly investigation + Explain/Defend + checkpoints fit accepted weights?
- checkpoints only Weeks 6, 9, 14?
- no due/late/drop policy invented?
- Friday receipts require interpretation?

### Instructor experience

- Monday recording plans usable?
- Stack Showcases authentic and relevant?
- premium/frontier hardware/tools clearly optional for students?

### Semester boundary

- Week 14 ends technical Architecture?
- Week 15 has no hidden new theory?
- Week 16 remains Farkle/ML shared fun, not Checkpoint 4?
- Week 17 uses frozen evidence only?

## Final completion test

> **What would you build now, and what evidence changed or strengthened your mind?**

Then inspect whether a reasonably diligent student could answer from the course they actually experienced.
