# Prompt 004_l - Cross-week continuity and editorial audit

**Status:** OPEN  
**Depends on:** 004_b through 004_k authored  
**Purpose:** prove the technical core is one course, not ten accepted directories

## Why this prompt exists

Individual week acceptance is necessary and insufficient.

A course can contain ten good weeks and still feel like a shuffled playlist. 004_l reads Weeks 5-14 as a student experiences them and repairs the seams.

This is the **death-by-competent-fragmentation detector**.

## Mission

Perform a complete cross-week editorial/continuity pass over the technical runway.

Do not primarily add content. Prefer deletion, consolidation, reuse, renaming, and clearer handoffs.

## Required audits

### 1. Machine continuity

Verify:

- Week 5 designed machine is still recognizable in Week 14;
- observable-machine evidence is used responsibly;
- workload/budget assumptions do not silently mutate;
- dated market evidence is clearly distinguished from durable doctrine.

### 2. Specimen continuity, Weeks 6-9

Verify one bounded program/value family survives representation -> datapath -> performance -> integration.

Hunt gratuitous new toy programs.

### 3. Memory continuity, Weeks 10-11

Week 11 should grow from Week 10's memory/data story rather than resetting into an OS lecture.

### 4. Workload continuity, Weeks 12-13

Week 13 specialization should feel like a new architecture answer to a workload question students already understand.

### 5. Metric maturation

Trace how naive Week 5 metrics become more sophisticated.

A student should see why:

- $/GB alone is incomplete;
- GHz alone is incomplete;
- core count alone is incomplete;
- accelerator headline numbers alone are incomplete.

Do not merely introduce new metrics. Show old ones becoming conditional.

### 6. Vocabulary timing

Find terms introduced before students need them, redefined inconsistently, or repeated excessively.

Prefer one good definition tied to evidence and later reuse.

### 7. Monday -> Wednesday -> Friday integrity

For every week ask:

- Does Monday provide the model needed for Wednesday?
- Does Wednesday generate evidence Friday actually uses?
- Can Friday be answered without doing Wednesday? If yes, fix it.

### 8. Dossier integrity

Verify the dossier is a living technical record, not a worksheet stack.

Remove artificial handoffs.

Confirm Week 14 can cite/reuse earlier evidence without reconstruction.

### 9. AI Fluency integrity

Ensure AI activities develop transferable judgment and do not become vendor-specific product exercises.

### 10. Professional Minds load

Ensure the mapped strand is present but does not create a second Architecture assignment or awkward forced metaphor.

### 11. Stack Showcase relevance

Every showcase must answer the week's course question with authentic evidence.

Delete/replace showcases that are cool product tours with no curricular payoff.

### 12. Source coherence

Required student reading should remain course-owned/humane. External sources verify/deepen, not carry the entire explanation.

### 13. Calendar/load coherence

Look for workload spikes, especially checkpoint weeks and Fall Break.

Do not solve workload problems by moving burden invisibly to another day.

### 14. Week 14 hard stop

Verify no required technical concept is deferred to Week 15-17 and no hidden Checkpoint 4 exists.

## Editorial questions

For each week record:

- what can be deleted;
- what can be reused from previous week;
- what handoff needs one sentence of clarification;
- what term/metric needs alignment;
- what artifact should remain persistent;
- what feels like a reset.

## Required artifact

Create a concise continuity map showing the persistent threads across Weeks 5-14 and the exact artifact handed from one week to the next.

Update authored weeks where necessary.

## Report

Write `sidecar/reports/004_l_cross_week_continuity_editorial_audit.md`.

Include before/after problems repaired and any remaining continuity YELLOWs.

## Done when

A reviewer can read the technical core in order and feel one accumulating investigation:

> build -> open -> trace -> stress -> explain -> redesign

rather than ten topics that happen to share a course number.
