# A6 - Professional Pathway Artifacts

## Purpose

Use the professional/career evidence you began in the shared Week 1 success-foundations work. Do not recreate an artifact merely because the semester is ending. **Update what changed, preserve what is still valid, and explain why.**

This assignment owns the two Architecture professional-pathway submissions in Weeks 14 and 15. The universal Week 1 baseline remains owned by `semester_kickoff_week`.

## Week 14 update - graded 5%

By the technical finale, revisit the career evidence that matters for your next step. Update as needed:

- target role or role family;
- skill-gap analysis;
- resume;
- GitHub/portfolio/LinkedIn or equivalent professional profile;
- degree/course plan where the semester changed your thinking;
- one Architecture artifact or capability you can now describe honestly.

Submit a short changelog explaining:

1. what changed since Week 1;
2. what evidence caused the change;
3. what you deliberately kept unchanged and why;
4. one Architecture skill/project signal you can now support with real evidence.

Polish matters less than an honest evidence trail.

## Week 15 completion - graded 5%

Week 15 is asynchronous wind-down, not a new technical project.

Submit the completed professional-pathway package using the artifacts that genuinely apply to you. A normal package may include:

- transcript/degree audit or current progress evidence;
- degree plan;
- resume;
- professional profile/portfolio links or exported evidence;
- target-role research;
- skill-gap analysis;
- brief next-steps reflection.

Your next-steps reflection should answer:

> Based on the evidence I have now, what should I do next and why?

## Reuse rule

Bring an existing artifact when it already satisfies the requirement. Explain the update or equivalence rather than manufacturing churn.

## Integrity rule

Do not claim a skill, project, benchmark, system, profile update, or career result that the submitted evidence cannot support. AI may help revise wording; the underlying evidence must remain yours and verifiable.
