# A7 - Final Reflection: What Can You See Now?

## Purpose

This reflection closes Computer Architecture in place of adding another technical exam/project sprint.

The Machine Dossier froze in Week 14. Week 17 asks you to **read the evidence you already made** and explain what you understand now that you could not explain at the beginning of the semester.

## Choose five prompts

Answer five prompts total, with at least one from each category. Use concrete evidence from your Machine Dossier, lab receipts, plots, traces, code/binary evidence, Week 16 Farkle receipt, or other course-produced work when it strengthens the answer.

### Seeing the machine

- What can you now explain about what happens underneath a program that you could not explain in August?
- Which machine observation, trace, or experiment made an abstraction become physical for you?
- Which Architecture word changed from a definition you memorized into something you can recognize in evidence?

### Evidence and judgment

- Which prediction were you most wrong about, and what evidence changed your mind?
- Which measurement taught you that a simple performance story was incomplete?
- Where did you learn to separate observation from interpretation?

### Design and tradeoffs

- Revisit one Week 5 machine/design choice. Why would you keep it or change it now?
- Which tradeoff in your final Machine Dossier matters most for the workload you chose?
- When did "more" hardware, more parallelism, more optimization, or more computation stop being automatically better?

### Tools, AI, and professional growth

- Describe one time AI/tool output was useful but needed independent verification.
- What reproducibility habit will you carry into another technical course or job?
- What kind of systems thinker/problem-solver are you becoming, and what is your next step?

## Closing requirement

Finish with one paragraph beginning:

> **At the start of the semester I thought a computer was... Now I think a computer is...**

Specific and honest reflection matters more than polish. Do not turn Week 17 into a retrospective feature sprint or rebuild the frozen dossier.
