## Method
- Grade = Canvas current score. Work = share of published, graded, points>0 assignments and discussions past due that have a turned-in submission (score never used; a zero with nothing turned in counts as not done). Hours = enrollment total_activity_time / 3600.
- Curve drawn between the 10th and 90th percentile of x. No points, labels or per-student ticks.

## Caveats
- Small class: a few students move these numbers a lot.
- The Fall gradebook has a known bug that once gave full credit to empty submissions.
- Hours is a proxy (time with pages open), not study time.
- Outliers dominate small samples.
- 31 past-due items (class-wide) carry credit but nothing turned in; per the rule they count as not done. They may be in-class work credited by hand or the gradebook bug, so the work axis may read low.
