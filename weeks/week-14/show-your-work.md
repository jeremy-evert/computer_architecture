# Show Your Work (Week 14, optional, no new deliverable)

By the end of this course you will have made something worth showing. This week connects to the Machine Dossier freeze: you are defending design choices with dated evidence, and a stranger reading the dossier needs to see where each claim came from.

Read the one-page guide first: https://jeremy-evert.github.io/swosu-computing/show-your-work/

What to look for this week:

- **Credit it:** every price, benchmark and source in the dossier carries its date and where it came from.
- **Do not overclaim:** words like fastest, best or cheapest need evidence you can point to.
- **Say how you made it:** name the tools you used, AI included, and what you checked yourself.
- **Test it as a stranger:** can someone who has not seen your machine follow the dossier from the first page?

Before you publish anything, tick the checklist: https://github.com/jeremy-evert/oeqa_spell_roller_public/blob/main/CHECKLIST.md

Pick one thing you made, find the first checklist box it fails, and fix that one box. That is the whole task.
