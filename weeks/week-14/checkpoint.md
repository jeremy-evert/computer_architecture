# Week 14 Checkpoint 3 - Final Machine Dossier defense

## Minimum defense

Defend at least **four consequential design choices**. At least **three** must cite course-produced technical evidence from different parts of Weeks 6-13.

A choice may be:

- changed from Week 5; or
- deliberately unchanged, now defended with stronger evidence.

Both can earn full credit.

## Rubric axes

- **Functions / Runs**
- **Concept Use**
- **Explanation**
- **Demonstrability / Reproducibility**
- **Tradeoff / Decision Quality**

Do not create a new grading category. This is the accepted Week 14 Machine Dossier checkpoint.

## AI/automation boundary

Automation may collect evidence, rebuild plots/tables, assemble the PDF, and check consistency. The final architecture judgment remains the student's.

## Freeze

After Checkpoint 3, the technical Dossier is frozen. No Architecture Checkpoint 4 exists.
