## Primary / official truth

Use the official page for the **exact part you select** whenever a compatibility or architecture claim matters.

Representative official lookup surfaces:

- **AMD Processor Specifications:** `https://www.amd.com/en/products/specifications/processors.html` - cores/threads, cache, socket, memory support, PCIe generation, power and other CPU properties.
- **Intel product specifications / comparison:** `https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/products/compare.html` - processor cores/threads, cache, frequency/power and product-specific properties.
- **NVIDIA GeForce comparison/specifications:** `https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/graphics-cards/compare/` - GPU memory configuration, power, architecture and current family specifications.
- **Exact motherboard/memory/storage/GPU/PSU/case manufacturer page** - use the product maker's page/manual for socket, memory generation, slot/interface, dimensions, power/connectors, or other compatibility facts.

The manufacturer page is strongest for **what the product is**. It is not automatically the strongest evidence for independent performance claims.

## Current market evidence

For price:

- use the retailer/aggregator price you actually observed;
- record exact model, USD price, URL, and date;
- do not silently substitute MSRP for an unavailable street price;
- Week 14 will refresh the market snapshot.

### PCPartPicker / builder surfaces

A PC builder such as `https://pcpartpicker.com/` may be used to assemble a candidate system and surface compatibility concerns.

It is **optional convenience** rather than required course truth. During the 2026-08-16 authoring validation pass, automated retrieval of PCPartPicker returned HTTP 403, which is one reason the assignment keeps a course-owned compatibility checklist and accepts equivalent workflows.
