# Week 6 Checkpoint 1

## Purpose

Follow **one value/operation** from the persistent C specimen through at least four evidence layers:

1. source meaning;
2. representation;
3. RISC-V instruction/disassembly;
4. architectural register/memory state.

Submit a concise trace with reproducible evidence, not binary worksheets.

## Accepted rubric axes

- **Functions / Runs**
- **Concept Use**
- **Explanation**
- **Demonstrability / Reproducibility**

The checkpoint uses the week's ordinary evidence and does not create a new grading category.

## AI boundary

AI may generate a candidate explanation or trace. Your submitted chain must point to independently verifiable course evidence.
