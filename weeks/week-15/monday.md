# Monday - Curate what mattered

There is no new Architecture lesson this week.

Look back across your semester evidence and choose **three artifacts** that changed, corrected, or strengthened your understanding. They might be:

- a machine snapshot;
- RISC-V/binary trace;
- dependency or pipeline evidence;
- memory plot;
- scaling/communication plot;
- vectorization result;
- Machine Dossier design decision;
- Week 16 Farkle cost/effectiveness receipt later in the semester.

For each selected artifact, write one sentence:

> I would keep this evidence because it changed/strengthened my understanding of ____ by showing ____.

This is curation, not a new submission system unless the Savnac assignment explicitly asks for the note. Its main purpose is to make Week 17 reflection easier and more evidence-backed.
