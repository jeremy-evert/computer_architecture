# Friday - Explain what changed after verification

Turn Wednesday's receipt into a short evidence-backed explanation.

## Explain / Defend receipt

Answer all five:

1. **Claim:** What was the bounded claim you investigated?
2. **Evidence:** What observation mattered most?
3. **Verdict:** What does the evidence support?
4. **Limitation:** What can you *not* conclude from that evidence?
5. **Revision:** What changed between the original proposal and your final explanation?

If AI helped, add one sentence identifying what the model/tool contributed and how you independently checked it.

## Strong shape

> The assistant proposed ____. On this execution environment, ____ reported ____. That evidence supports ____ but does not establish ____. I therefore revised the original claim to ____.

The goal is not to catch AI being wrong. The goal is to become the person who knows **why** a technical claim deserves trust.
