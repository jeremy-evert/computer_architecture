# Friday - What did the environment actually control?

Use Wednesday's two-run receipt to make one bounded reproducibility claim.

Answer:

1. Which parts of the experiment were fixed by the course source/wrapper?
2. Which facts describe the environment that actually executed the command?
3. Which facts could still differ on another computer?
4. What evidence would another person need to decide whether a difference is meaningful?
5. What would you change in your original reproduction plan?

A strong conclusion does **not** say "the results are identical, therefore the machines are identical."

A stronger shape is:

> The same course source and command produced the same required evidence shape across repeated runs. The receipt records the executing environment, while host hardware and timing may still differ. Another person can reproduce the reasoning task by preserving ____ and recording ____.
