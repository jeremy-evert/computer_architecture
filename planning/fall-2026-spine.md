# COMSC-3013 Fall 2026 semester spine

**Status:** accepted curriculum spine. This file names the durable week sequence; `block-map.md` owns the full M/W/F AI Fluency + Professional Minds + lecture/lab/showcase production map.

## Course arc

**Weeks 1-4:** build the investigator.  
**Weeks 5-9:** build and open the machine.  
**Weeks 10-13:** stress the machine.  
**Week 14:** become the architect and finish the technical course.  
**Weeks 15-17:** wind down, play/apply, reflect.

## Week map

| Week | Dates | Theme | Central question / purpose | Major persistent evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aug 17-21 | **Success Foundations** | How do I survive this semester, thrive in the degree, and enjoy the career? | Universal human-orientation work; no Architecture gate. |
| 2 | Aug 24-28 | **AI Lab Training** | How can AI help investigate without becoming the source of truth? | First investigation/verification receipt. |
| 3 | Aug 31-Sep 4 | **Containers & Repeatability** | How can another machine/person reproduce the experiment? | Reproducibility receipt. |
| 4 | Sep 8-11 | **Linux as a Machine Telescope** | How do I ask the machine what it is doing? | Machine-observation receipt. |
| 5 | Sep 14-18 | **Build the Machine** | What should I build for this workload, what does each part buy me, and how should I compare choices? | Machine Dossier v0; PC build + hierarchy/economics. |
| 6 | Sep 21-25 | **Bits Become Instructions** | What must software and hardware agree on for a program to run? | **Checkpoint 1:** representation/ISA evidence chain. |
| 7 | Sep 28-Oct 2 | **Crack Open the CPU** | What has to exist inside the CPU for one instruction to execute correctly? | Datapath/control trace/build evidence. |
| 8 | Oct 5-9 | **Make It Fast Without Breaking It** | Why does overlap improve performance, and why does it create new problems? | Pipeline/performance evidence + first sensitivity plot. |
| 9 | Oct 12-14 | **Follow the Program Down** | Can I connect source, representation, ISA, and processor behavior without hand-waving? | **Checkpoint 2:** full-stack integration. |
| 10 | Oct 19-23 | **Make the Memory Hierarchy Hurt** | Why do we need layers of memory, and what does crossing a layer feel like? | Cache/locality/latency/bandwidth sensory plots. |
| 11 | Oct 26-30 | **The Useful Lie of Memory** | What hardware mechanisms create the world a process thinks it sees? | VM/protection/I/O mechanism evidence. |
| 12 | Nov 2-6 | **More Cores, More Problems** | When does adding workers help, and when does cooperation cost more than it buys? | Scaling/communication sensitivity plot. |
| 13 | Nov 9-13 | **Different Machines for Different Work** | When does workload shape justify specialization? | CPU/vector/GPU/accelerator workload-fit evidence. |
| 14 | Nov 16-20 | **Sit in the Architect's Chair** | What would I build now, and what evidence changed or strengthened my decision? | **Checkpoint 3:** final Machine Dossier redesign/defense; dossier freezes. |
| 15 | Nov 23-27 | **Thanksgiving Wind-Down** | What evidence should I preserve and what needs to be caught up? | Curation/catch-up only; no new Architecture theory. |
| 16 | Nov 30-Dec 4 | **Farkle + Machine Learning** | What does this shared workload make me notice now that I know how to look? | Shared application/fun evidence only; no Architecture checkpoint. |
| 17 | Dec 7-11 | **Reflection** | What can I explain now that I could not explain in August? | Final evidence-backed reflection using frozen dossier/labs. |

## Checkpoint rhythm

- **Week 6:** Checkpoint 1 - light first synthesis.
- **Week 9:** Checkpoint 2 - full-stack source-to-CPU integration.
- **Week 14:** Checkpoint 3 - final Machine Dossier and evidence-backed redesign.
- **Week 16:** no Checkpoint 4.

## Hard ending rule

Computer Architecture technical instruction **ends at Week 14**.

Week 15 does not add theory.  
Week 16 does not reopen the dossier.  
Week 17 does not add content.

That winding-down structure is intentional and shared with the broader Fall 2026 course family.
