# Week 5 — Bits Become Meaning (Sep 14–18)

## Status
Accepted spine; detailed open-source references, lab, and validation still need authoring.

## Weekly Focus
How can the same bits become numbers, characters, addresses, instructions, and decisions? Build an intuitive and quantitative foundation for representation, logic, and arithmetic.

This is the first half of the **Meaning + Contract** sister pair with Week 6.

## Monday — Sep 14 — Frame
Representation as interpretation: binary/hex, unsigned and signed values, two's complement, overflow, floating-point approximation, and why software types ultimately become bit patterns.

## Wednesday — Sep 16 — Inspect / Build / Measure
Manipulate and observe real representations. Candidate activity shape: inspect values in memory/binaries, change values and compare bytes, explore overflow/precision, and trace a small Boolean/ALU operation.

## Friday — Sep 18 — Explain / Defend
Explain what a chosen bit pattern means under more than one interpretation and identify a representation limit or design tradeoff using evidence.

## Evidence this week
A representation investigation showing value → bits/bytes → interpretation, including at least one observed limitation such as overflow or floating-point approximation.

## Open authoring notes
Author Weeks 5 and 6 together enough that the same tiny values/programs continue from representation into ISA. Avoid a conversion-worksheet week detached from real machine evidence.
